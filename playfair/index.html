INTRODUCTION

What is the Playfair Cipher?

Playfair encrypts plaintext two letters at a time, treating each pair as a digraph. A keyword is used to construct a 5 × 5 matrix, with I/J sharing one cell.

🔢

Digraph Encryption

Two plaintext letters are processed together instead of one at a time.

⬜

5 × 5 Matrix

The keyword and remaining alphabet form a 25-cell Playfair matrix.

🔑

Keyword Based

The keyword determines the order of letters in the cipher matrix.

ALGORITHM

Rules of the Playfair Cipher

These are the rules implemented by the website.

01

Repeated letters

When both letters of a digraph are the same, insert a filler letter, normally X, between them.

BALLOON → BA LX LO ON
02

Same row

Replace each letter by the letter immediately to its right. The row wraps around at the end.

Right shift → circular wrap
03

Same column

Replace each letter by the letter immediately below it. The column wraps around at the bottom.

Down shift → circular wrap
04

Rectangle rule

For letters in different rows and columns, keep each row and exchange the columns.

HS → BP in the MONARCHY matrix
HISTORY

A Classical Encryption Technique

Playfair was historically used as a field cipher and was used by the British Army during World War I and by Allied forces during World War II. It is more resistant to simple single-letter frequency analysis because it operates on digraphs, although it is not considered secure by modern standards.
